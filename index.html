How it works

One connection. More projects.

A factory's grid connection is sized for its peak, and most of the time a large part of it sits unused. We find that spare capacity and plug new projects into it.

  1. 01

    Find

    We read the site's connection agreement and a year of 15-minute load data to see how much capacity is free, and when.

  2. 02

    Match

    We score batteries, solar, data centres and other energy projects against the site: capacity, space, voltage level, location and timing.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Both sides sign, a controller keeps the site within its contracted capacity, and the grid operator is notified. No new connection.
